# Inventory and Replenishment Policy

## Stockout
A stockout occurs when available inventory for a product reaches zero or otherwise becomes unavailable for sale.

## Reorder Point
The inventory level at which replenishment should normally be initiated. The numerical threshold is product- or category-specific and should come from operational data or configuration.

## Safety Stock
Inventory held to protect against demand variability, supply delays, and forecast error.

## Inventory Turnover
A measure of how efficiently inventory is converted into sales over a defined period. The formula and inventory valuation convention should be stated when presenting the metric.

## Replenishment Analysis
Recommendations may consider demand, current inventory, inbound supply, lead time, and safety-stock requirements where those fields are available.

## BI Guidance
Use PostgreSQL for numerical inventory and sales data. Use this document to interpret business terminology.
